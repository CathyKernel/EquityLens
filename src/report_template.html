  • Limitations. Directional accuracy above the coin-flip level is modest and consistent with weak-form market efficiency; the edge is thin and sensitive to cost assumptions. The universe is limited to 21 large caps, labels are close-dated with no overnight-gap separation, and no regime-switching or macro features are included. This is a research artefact demonstrating methodology, not investment advice.
